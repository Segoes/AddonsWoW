# Changelog

## 1.0.0
First public release.
- Window with the fishing skill bar, Equip pole / Swap back and Apply lure buttons.
- One-key fishing: the same key casts and reels in.
- Louder splash while fishing, and an alert when the fishing skill goes up.
- When fishing fails, the reason shows in red in chat, in the addon language.
- Fishing pools recorded on catch and shown on the world map and minimap.
- Pools sent by players through the form ship with the addon; export your own with `/anglers export`.
- A daily reminder at login when you have pools not yet on the shared map.
- English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese and Italian.
